Start here: the water analogy

Every page uses the same idea: electricity behaves like water flowing in pipes. Keep this table in mind and the rest gets much easier.

Water
Electricity
Pump
→
Battery / power supply
Pressure
→
Voltage, in volts (V)
Flow of water
→
Current, in amps (A)
Narrow pipe
→
Resistance, in ohms (Ω)
River (one direction, steady)
→
DC: Direct Current
Waves sloshing back and forth
→
AC: Alternating Current

And the one formula that ties them together: V = I × R, which in water terms is pressure = flow × narrowness.

How to use these pages

  • Every page has the same structure: short version → what it's for → water analogy → interactive demos → example circuits → key facts → recap.
  • Play with every slider and button. Breaking things (bursting capacitors, burning LEDs) is part of learning, and nothing here costs money!
  • Got a question? Ask, and the explanation gets added to the page.
