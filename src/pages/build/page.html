How to use the lab

🧲

Place

Drag a part from the catalog onto the board: it snaps to the most logical holes. Drag it again to move it, R to rotate, Del to remove.

🔌

Wire

Press on a hole (or a leg) and drag to another hole. Pick the colour first in the toolbar, or click a wire to change it.

▶

Play

The circuit comes alive: LEDs light, motors turn. Hover a hole to read its voltage. Too much current? The part burns: click it and press Replace.

✓

Check

Lists the usual mistakes: a short circuit, an LED without resistor, a part with both legs in the same strip, a loose leg…

The breadboard works like the real one: in each column, holes a–e are connected together, and so are f–j. The four long rails (+ and − at the top and bottom) run along the whole board. Open a project from the Circuits page with its 🔧 Build it button.
