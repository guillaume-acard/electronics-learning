How to use the lab

🧲

Place

Drag a part from the catalog onto the board: it snaps to the most logical holes. Drag its body to move it, or drag one leg of a 2-leg part (resistor, LED, capacitor…) to another hole. R to rotate, Del to remove. Click a part to change its value or model (diode 1N4007 or 1N4148, ceramic or electrolytic capacitor…).

🔌

Wire

Press on a free hole (next to a leg, in the same strip) and drag to another hole. Pick the colour first in the toolbar, or click a wire to change it. To move a wire, drag one of its ends to another hole. To remove a part or a wire, drag it back onto the parts list.

▶

Play

The circuit comes alive: LEDs light, motors turn, counters count, the speaker plays. Hover a hole to read its voltage. Too much current? The part burns: click it and press Replace.

📍

Probes

In the Multimeter card, press Probe 1 (or 2), then click a hole: its chart shows the voltage over time. Click a part instead to see the current through it. Watch a capacitor charge, the 555 square wave, a tone.

✓

Check

Lists the usual mistakes: a short circuit, an LED without resistor, a part with both legs in the same strip, a loose leg…

The breadboard works like the real one: in each column, holes a–e are connected together, and so are f–j. The four long rails (+ and − at the top and bottom) run along the whole board. Open a project from the Circuits page with its 🔧 Build it button.
