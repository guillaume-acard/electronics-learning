★How to use this page

Pick a project in the menu on the left: they are grouped by theme, and within a theme they go from easiest to hardest. Each one is a small circuit you can build on a breadboard in 10–20 minutes, to see what the lesson explained. Every card lists the parts, the build steps and variants to try, with the result you should observe. The schematics are animated to show what to expect.

🛒 Need the parts? See the Gotronic shopping list with every component for these projects and the real-life builds, with codes, minimum quantities (one circuit built at a time) and a live total (≈ €106 for everything required).

Basic kit: breadboard, jumper wires, 9 V battery + clip, assorted resistors, LEDs, capacitors, a few BC547 transistors, 1N4007 diodes and a multimeter. Everything here is low voltage (≤ 12 V) and safe.

🗺️ Breadboard map used in the wiring guides

  • Rails: the long lines at the top and bottom. Red = +, blue = −. On some boards the rails are split in the middle: bridge them with a jumper.
  • Columns are numbered 1, 2, 3… The 5 holes a–e of one column are connected together, and so are f–j. The two halves are not connected across the centre gap.
  • Chips straddle the gap with the notch on the left: pin 1 is bottom-left, pins go left→right along the bottom, then right→left along the top.
  • BC547 flat side facing you: legs C B E from left to right. LED: long leg = +. Electrolytic capacitor: stripe = −.
