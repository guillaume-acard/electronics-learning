#CD4017 decade counter: the LED chaser

What it does: it has 10 outputs. Only one is on at a time. Each pulse on the clock pin moves the "on" to the next output: 0, 1, 2 … 9, then back to 0.

How it works inside: a chain of flip-flops (tiny 1-bit memories made of transistors) counts the pulses, and a decoder turns the count into "light output number N". Connect an output to the reset pin and it starts over early, so it counts to any number from 2 to 10.

Water analogy: a rotating distributor valve that sends water to the next of 10 pipes each time you push its lever. The classic pairing: a 555 provides the clock pulses and the 4017 counts them.
