#74HC595 shift register: 8 outputs from 3 wires

What it does: a microcontroller often doesn't have enough pins. The 595 lets you control 8 outputs with just 3 wires. Chain several and you get 16, 24 or more, still with 3 wires.

How it works inside (3 steps):

  1. Put a bit (1 or 0) on the DATA wire.
  2. Pulse the CLOCK wire. Every bit inside shifts one place along, and the new bit enters at the start. Repeat 8 times.
  3. Pulse the LATCH wire. All 8 bits are copied to the outputs at the same moment, so the LEDs don't flicker while bits are moving.

Water analogy: a bucket brigade. Cups of water (1) or empty cups (0) are passed down a line of 8 people, one step per clock. When the line is full, everyone pours into their own tank at the same time (the latch).

Click the LEDs at the bottom to choose a pattern, then press Send.
