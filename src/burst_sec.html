Try it: push past the limit

This capacitor is 1000 µF, rated 16 V. Move the battery voltage slider and watch the rubber sheet.

  • Below 16 V: the sheet stretches, the flow stops, and it's fully charged. A higher voltage just means more stretch and more charge.
  • Near 16 V: the sheet is under strain. A tiny leak appears, and in real life the capacitor wears out faster.
  • Above 16 V: the sheet is overstretched. After a moment it tears, and water then flows straight through the hole non-stop. That isn't charging, it's a broken capacitor acting like a short circuit.
